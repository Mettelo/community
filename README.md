# Mettelo Community

Welcome to the Mettelo Community.

This repository serves as the central hub for everything related to our global data and AI community. Whether you're just starting your career or already working in the industry, this is where you can connect, collaborate, contribute, and grow.

## What you'll find here

- Community guidelines
- Member onboarding
- Events and workshops
- Community projects
- Learning challenges
- Mentorship programmes
- Volunteer opportunities
- Job and referral initiatives
- Announcements and updates

We welcome:

- Data Analysts

- AI Engineers
- Business Intelligence Developers
- Students and Career Switchers
- Researchers

Every contribution helps strengthen the community.
